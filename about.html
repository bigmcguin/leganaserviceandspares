A local workshop for the West Tamar

Legana Service & Spares is locally owned and operated, looking after the cars, 4WDs, caravans and trailers of Legana and the surrounding area.

One place for the whole job

Most vehicle jobs need more than one thing: a service turns up worn brakes, a roadworthy needs a new tyre, a battery needs replacing on the spot. We handle servicing, repairs, inspections and parts in the one workshop, so you deal with one team and one bill.

We're a TACC accredited repairer, a licensed AIS inspection station and an RACT roadside agent, which means the work meets Tasmanian industry standards and we can help you get back on the road when something goes wrong.
